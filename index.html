ASTROPHYSICS · GALAXY EVOLUTION · EXTRAGALACTIC ASTRONOMY

Exploring how galaxies form, evolve, and transform across cosmic time.

I’m an astrophysicist from Colombia 🇨🇴 — a physicist from the Universidad de Nariño, in the surprising city of Pasto, and an MSc in Astrophysics from the Institute of Radio Astronomy and Astrophysics (IRyA–UNAM), in the charming city of Morelia, Mexico.

My research focuses on the spatially resolved properties of stellar populations and the interstellar medium in nearby galaxies, combining integral-field spectroscopy with multiwavelength observations.

More recently, I have become particularly interested in dust attenuation and its role in understanding galaxies across cosmic time, using observations from the James Webb Space Telescope.

I’m fascinated by the possibility of using these observations to uncover what galaxies were like in the early Universe — and hopefully find a few stunning things along the way.

Astronomy is my passion, and science is what I genuinely enjoy doing. Yes, I’m a bit of a nerd. 🤓
