01 · ABOUT ME

Astronomy is my passion.

I’m an astrophysicist from Colombia 🇨🇴 — MSc in Astrophysics from the Institute of Radio Astronomy and Astrophysics (IRyA–UNAM), in the charming city of Morelia, Mexico.

My current research focuses mainly on the spatially resolved properties of stellar populations and the interstellar medium in nearby galaxies, combining integral-field spectroscopy with multiwavelength observations.

More recently, I have worked in dust attenuation and its role in understanding galaxies across cosmic time, using observations from the James Webb Space Telescope.

I’m fascinated by the possibility of using these observations to uncover what galaxies were like in the early Universe — and hopefully find a few stunning things along the way.

Science is what I genuinely enjoy doing (nerd).
